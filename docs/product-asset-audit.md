# SAMAY Product Asset Audit

## Meridian detail evidence

The local archive contains a matched Meridian ivory-dial close-up and an exhibition caseback image. Both show the existing brushed-steel, ivory-dial, dark-calfskin Meridian reference. They are suitable for additional gallery evidence and product inspection, but do **not** evidence an alternate configuration.

## Serein and Vesper detail evidence

The Serein bracelet study shows the existing steel, slate-dial, integrated-bracelet reference. The Vesper caseback study shows the existing dark leather-strapped technical reference. Both are suitable as additional gallery evidence for their respective photographed configurations; neither demonstrates an alternate configuration.

## Durable gallery assignment

The verified Meridian dial, Meridian caseback, Serein bracelet, and Vesper caseback studies are stored in project-managed durable media and are assigned to their respective reference galleries. The configuration selector remains single-option until original exterior photography establishes a genuinely distinct configuration.

## Verified inventory decision

The Meridian dial and caseback, Serein bracelet, and Vesper caseback files are retained as inspection evidence only. They must not be relabelled as new editions, colours, materials, straps, or availability states. SAMAY’s product data therefore continues to present one evidence-backed configuration per reference.

## Activation rule

Only original full-object images that unambiguously show a separate case, dial, or strap configuration may activate a selectable product variant. Detail, movement, and crop studies remain gallery frames for the existing photographed configuration.
